0.2.0-test1 - first mobile browser prototype
- Phone-sized glass controls and collapsible sections.
- Bottom toolbar for settings, pause, next preset, fullscreen and help.
- Drag attraction/repulsion, tap bursts, pinch zoom, two-finger rotation.
- Performance presets with frame-rate, resolution and shape-count limits.
- Separate saved settings; playlist remains manually controlled.
- Keeps the existing images, audio, layers, morphing and palette fixes.
- No Windows changes, APK, operating-system wallpaper or store submission.
